Cooking with your iPhone

Cook mode on iPhone: keep the screen on

Quick fix without an app: Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock > Never. That keeps the screen on in every app, uses more battery, and you have to remember to switch it back after dinner. In ReciPics the screen stays on only while a recipe is open, and the steps can be read aloud.

What ReciPics does while a recipe is open

  • The screen stays on as long as the recipe is open. When you leave the recipe, your normal Auto-Lock applies again.
  • Tap Read next to Recipe Steps, and ReciPics reads all steps aloud with the iPhone's built-in voice for your device language. Tap it again to stop.
  • Tap an ingredient to tick it off once it is in the bowl.
  • Steps are open by default. Collapse the ones you have finished.
  • Switch servings between 1x, 2x and 4x before you start.

How to use it

  1. Save the recipe in ReciPics: paste a link from a recipe site, use the Share button, or photograph a recipe card or cookbook page.
  2. Open the recipe from your cookbook and put the iPhone where you can see it.
  3. Tap Read to hear the steps, and tick off ingredients as you go.

A question halfway through? Ask

The sparkle button in a recipe opens the assistant. Type or say your question, for example what to do if the sauce is too thin or if you don't have a whisk. Answers are about this recipe, and you can have them read aloud. If the assistant changes the recipe, you can undo the change.

Questions

Does iPhone have a built-in cook mode?

Not as a separate setting. iPhone has Auto-Lock, which applies to all apps. Some apps keep the screen on by themselves while you cook; ReciPics does this while a recipe is open.

Does it read one step at a time?

No. Read reads all steps one after another. Tap Read again to stop. There are no voice commands such as “next step”.

Which voice does it use?

The iPhone's built-in speech voice for your device language. Reading aloud happens on your iPhone; the recipe is not sent anywhere for it.

Can I use it with recipes from anywhere?

With any recipe in your ReciPics cookbook: recipes saved from links, from photos of cards or cookbook pages, and recipes the app suggests. Some websites don't allow importing.

Is ReciPics only for iPhone?

Yes. ReciPics is an iPhone app for iOS 26 or later. There is no Android or web version.
