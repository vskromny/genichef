Shopping for a recipe

A grocery list from your recipes

Copying ingredients from a recipe onto a shopping note, one by one, takes time. In ReciPics, open a recipe and add its ingredients to your grocery list from the recipe's menu. Tick items off while you shop and share the list as text or PDF.

From recipe to grocery list

  1. Open a recipe in ReciPics.
  2. Tap the menu (···) and choose “Add to Grocery List”.
  3. The recipe's ingredients appear in the Grocery List tab, grouped under the recipe name.

If you add the same recipe again, its items are updated instead of added twice. Ingredients from different recipes are not combined: if two recipes need onions, onions are listed under each recipe.

While you shop

  • Tick items off. Ticked items move to “Checked Items”, and “Clear Purchased” removes them.
  • Add things that are not in a recipe with +: name, quantity, unit and an optional note.
  • Group the list by recipe, or turn grouping off to see a single list. You can also add your own sections and rename or reorder them.
  • Share the list as text or PDF, or print it, for example to send it to whoever is doing the shopping.

The list is stored on your iPhone. It does not sync between devices, and there is no shared live list: sharing sends a copy as text or PDF.

Amounts and servings

The list uses the amounts as written in the recipe. The 1x, 2x and 4x picker changes the amounts shown in the recipe, not those on the list. For a bigger batch, change the amounts with “Edit recipe” before you add them, or add extra items by hand. For other amounts, use the recipe scaler in your browser.

Getting recipes in

  • Recipe websites: copy the link, tap “Paste a link” in ReciPics and paste it. Save recipes from websites
  • Cookbook pages and handwritten cards: take a photo in ReciPics, or choose one from your library.
  • Keep the recipes you cook often in your cookbook. A digital cookbook on your iPhone

What to expect

ReciPics reads recipes from photos and links with AI, and AI can make mistakes. Compare an imported recipe with the original before you cook, especially amounts and temperatures. ReciPics may add short step titles and suggested ingredient alternatives (shown as “alternative”). These are not from the original.

Check ingredients yourself for allergies or intolerances; ReciPics can misread or miss an ingredient.

Each photo scan uses the app's AI allowance, which is limited without Premium. For new users without a paid plan, the number of link imports per month is limited. The app shows how many you have left.

Recipes from cookbooks and websites are usually protected by copyright. Use ReciPics for your private cookbook only, and don't share or publish imported recipes you have no right to share. To read a recipe, the photo and the text recognized on your iPhone, or the link, are sent to our servers and service providers, including our AI provider OpenAI (USA); see the Privacy page.
