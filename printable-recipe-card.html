How-to: recipe card as PDF

Make a printable recipe card

Want a recipe on paper for the kitchen, or as a file to send to someone? Get it into ReciPics from a link, a photo or a screenshot, tidy it up, and share it as a PDF or print it straight from your iPhone.

Step by step

  1. Get the recipe into ReciPics: tap “Paste a link” for a website or a TikTok or Instagram post, or use a photo or screenshot of the recipe.
  2. Open the recipe and check it. Use “Edit recipe” in the menu (···) to fix amounts, rename it or shorten the steps.
  3. In the same menu, choose “Share as PDF” to send the card to Messages, Mail or Files, or “Print” to send it to an AirPrint printer.

What is on the card

  • The recipe title, the photo if the recipe has one, and a few details such as cuisine and difficulty.
  • The ingredient list and the steps, as they are in the app at that moment, including your edits.
  • A short footer line “Shared from ReciPics” with a link to the app.

Limits

  • One recipe per PDF. There is no cookbook or booklet export.
  • The PDF uses US Letter page size. On A4 paper, choose a fit-to-page option in the print dialog if the edges are cut off.
  • There are no card templates, fonts or index-card sizes to choose from. If you need a different layout, “Share as Text” gives you the recipe as text to paste into your own design.
  • Long recipes run onto a second page.

Getting a recipe in by link uses a link import. New installs without a paid plan get 5 free link imports per calendar month. Only imports that work count, and the import screen shows how many are left. Each photo scan uses the app's AI allowance, which is limited without Premium.

ReciPics reads recipes with AI, and AI can make mistakes. Compare the recipe with the original before you cook, especially amounts and temperatures, and check ingredients yourself for allergies or intolerances.
