Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2025

ReciPics is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

ReciPics does not store or collect personal data beyond the essential functionality of the app. The app may process the following:

  • Camera images (processed directly on OpenAI servers)
  • Voice inputs (processed directly on OpenAI servers)

2. Data Processing and Storage

  • ReciPics does not store, transmit, or retain camera images or voice inputs after they are processed by OpenAI.
  • No data is used for analytics, advertising, or any other secondary purpose.
  • OpenAI servers process images and text strictly for generating recipe results.

3. Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI: ReciPics relies on OpenAI for AI-driven recipe generation. Data sent to OpenAI is processed but not stored by ReciPics.
  • Vercel: Our backend functions solely to forward API requests, without retaining or analyzing any user data.

4. User Control and Data Rights

Since ReciPics does not store personal data, there is no need for data deletion requests. Users maintain full control over the device-stored aspects of the app.

5. Security Measures

ReciPics ensures that all API communications occur over encrypted channels to protect your privacy. We do not sell, share, or trade any user data with third parties.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected within the app.

7. Creator and Business Contacts (Partnership Outreach)

This section applies to creators and businesses we contact about a possible partnership with ReciPics. It does not apply to users of the app (see sections 1 to 5). It is provided under Article 14 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR and Article 19 of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG). We also link to this notice in our first message to you, and in any case within one month of obtaining your data.

  • Who is responsible (controller): Vladimir Skromny, operating ReciPics, contactable at hello@recipics.app.
  • What we process: your name, your public handle, the public business or contact email you list on your profile, bio or website, public content we quote when we write to you (for example captions or comments), our message history and your replies, and your opt-out status.
  • Where it comes from: your own publicly accessible profile, bio or website (for example on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube). We do not buy contact data.
  • Why: to contact you about an app partnership with ReciPics, and to keep a record of contacts and opt-outs so that we do not write to you again after you ask us not to.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in approaching creators and businesses about a partnership that promotes ReciPics (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR / EU GDPR). Under the revDSG, processing is justified by our overriding private interest in the same purpose (Article 31 revDSG). We balance this against your rights, contact you only at a business or public contact address, and stop when you object.
  • How long we keep it: contacts who do not reply are deleted 12 months after our last message to you; if you reply, we keep the conversation only while the discussion is active and then for up to 12 months. If you opt out, we keep only a minimal suppression entry (your handle and/or email address) so that we honour your request; our legitimate interest is in honouring your objection, and we keep it for as long as needed for that purpose.
  • Who receives it: we do not sell your data. It is handled only by the following providers, which act on our instructions as processors (or, for the social platforms, under their own terms):
    • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (Gmail / Google Workspace): sending and storing our emails.
    • Meta Platforms Ireland Limited (Instagram): direct messages sent through Instagram. The platform handles them under its own terms.
    • TikTok Technology Limited (Ireland) / TikTok Pte. Ltd.: direct messages sent through TikTok. The platform handles them under its own terms.
    • Apify Technologies s.r.o. (Czech Republic): collection of publicly accessible profile information and public posts and comments, used to research which creators may be a fit for a partnership.
    • Anthropic PBC (USA): AI assistance to assess the fit of a public profile and to draft messages, based on the public information described above.
  • International transfers: where a provider is located outside the UK, EU/EEA or Switzerland, we rely on an adequacy decision or on safeguards. Google LLC states that it complies with the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Frameworks (policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks), so transfers to Google in the US rely on those frameworks. Anthropic PBC (USA) does not state a Data Privacy Framework certification; its Data Processing Addendum incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses (Modules Two and Three) together with a Swiss Addendum, and we rely on those. Transfers to the EU/EEA, such as to Apify in the Czech Republic, are covered by the adequacy findings between those territories; Apify's data processing addendum notes that it may transfer personal data to the United States and to its own sub-processors, under its own transfer safeguards. For Instagram (Meta) and TikTok, see the next paragraph. You can request a copy of the safeguards at hello@recipics.app.
  • Instagram and TikTok messages: for messages you receive or send through Instagram or TikTok, those platforms process the data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies, not on our behalf. Meta states that it transfers data using the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and publishes a separate EU-U.S./Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework disclosure; TikTok states that it relies on adequacy decisions and Standard Contractual Clauses (and, absent those, Article 49 GDPR) and does not mention the Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights. You can ask us for access to your data, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing. You can object at any time to our processing on grounds relating to your particular situation, and you have an absolute right to object to direct marketing, after which we will stop. Under the revDSG these rights are set out in Articles 25 and 32. Data portability does not apply to this processing. To exercise any right, or to opt out, simply reply “stop” to any message from us or write to hello@recipics.app. You may also complain to a supervisory authority: the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, edoeb.admin.ch), or the data protection authority in your EU country. We carry out no automated decision-making or profiling with legal effect. In Switzerland you may also bring a claim before a civil court.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at hello@recipics.app.
