Fluffy Berry Pancakes

25 min · 2 servings · Easy

Thick, soft pancakes with yogurt, fresh berries and maple syrup. Ready in 25 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 250 ml milk
  • 30 g butter, melted, plus a little for the pan
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 150 g mixed berries (strawberries, blueberries)
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • Icing sugar, for dusting

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Beat the eggs with the milk and melted butter, pour into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and brush it with butter. Add about 3 tablespoons of batter per pancake.
  4. Cook until bubbles appear on top, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook 1 minute more.
  5. Stack the pancakes, top with yogurt and berries, drizzle with maple syrup and dust with icing sugar.
