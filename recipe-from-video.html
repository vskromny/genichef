How-to: recipe from a video

Turn a recipe video into a recipe card

Watched a cooking video where the recipe is only said out loud and not written in the caption? Paste the link into ReciPics. If the caption has no recipe, the app tries the video itself and writes what it picks up into a recipe with ingredients and steps. The result is often incomplete, so plan to check and fill in the amounts.

Step by step

  1. In TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on the video and choose “Copy link”.
  2. Open ReciPics and tap “Paste a link”. The copied link is filled in for you.
  3. Tap “Import”. ReciPics reads the caption first. If there is no recipe in it, you see “Reading the video…”, and the app works with part of what is said and some of the text shown on screen. This takes longer than a caption import.
  4. Open the recipe and play the video next to it. Fix or add amounts with “Edit recipe” in the menu (···).

What works, and what doesn't

  • Public videos up to 2 minutes long. Longer videos are not read.
  • Recipes taken from a video are marked “Extracted from the video — check quantities”, because spoken amounts are easy to get wrong.
  • If the recipe is only in the comments or behind a “link in bio”, and is not said or shown in the video, there is nothing to import.
  • Private accounts and posts that need a login can't be imported. Instagram and TikTok change often, so an import can fail or be paused; the app tells you when that happens.

What we tested: on 14 Nov 2026 we imported two of our own TikTok videos with no recipe in the caption. Both came in from the video and were marked for checking. The results were incomplete: in one, most amounts were left blank; in the other, one ingredient was missed. We have not yet tested such videos from Instagram.

If the video path misses too much, take a screenshot of the screen where the ingredients are shown and turn the screenshot into a recipe.

Limits

New installs without a paid plan get 5 free link imports per calendar month. Only imports that work count, and the import screen shows how many are left.

ReciPics reads recipes with AI, and AI can make mistakes. Compare the recipe with the original before you cook, especially amounts and temperatures, and check ingredients yourself for allergies or intolerances.

Recipe written in the caption? Then the normal link import is enough: Save recipes from Instagram and TikTok.
