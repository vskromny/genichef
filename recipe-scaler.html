Cups are US cups (about 237 ml), tbsp and tsp are US spoons (about 15 ml and 5 ml). oz means weight ounces; for liquids use fl oz. Results are rounded.

    

    

      
How it works

      
  • Amounts at the start of a line are scaled: whole numbers, decimals, fractions such as 1/2 or 1 1/2, and ½ ¼ ¾ ⅓ ⅔. Lines without a number stay as they are.
  • Cups, teaspoons and tablespoons are shown as fractions where the result is clean, other amounts as rounded decimals. Only the first amount in a line is scaled, so check the result before you cook, especially for baking.
  • The converter works within one kind of measure: volume to volume, weight to weight, temperature to temperature. How many grams a cup holds depends on the ingredient, so cups are not converted to grams.

    

    

      
Scan or import the recipe first

      
Is the recipe still in a cookbook or on a website? Add it to ReciPics first: take a photo of the page, or copy the link and tap “Paste a link”. In the app you can then scale servings (1x, 2x, 4x), add the ingredients to your grocery list and tap “Add to Cookbook” to keep the recipe.

      
Scan recipes from cookbooks · Save recipes from websites

      
ReciPics reads recipes from photos and links with AI, and AI can make mistakes. Compare an imported recipe with the original before you cook, especially amounts and temperatures.
