How-to: the Share button

Save a recipe on your iPhone from the Share button

Bookmarks and saved posts get lost. With ReciPics on your iPhone, you can send a recipe straight from the app you are in, such as Safari, TikTok or Instagram, to your cookbook through the iPhone share sheet. No copying and pasting.

Deutsch

Step by step

  1. Open the recipe page, post or video and tap Share. In Safari it is the square with the arrow. In TikTok and Instagram the system share sheet often sits behind an option such as “More” or “Share to…”.
  2. Choose ReciPics in the row of apps. ReciPics opens with the link already filled in.
  3. Tap “Import”. ReciPics reads the recipe and puts it in your cookbook with ingredients and steps.

ReciPics is not in the share sheet?

In the share sheet, scroll the app row to the end and tap “More”. Find ReciPics there and add it to your favorites so it shows up at the front next time.

No share sheet in the app you are using? Tap “Copy link” instead, then open ReciPics and tap “Paste a link”. The copied link is filled in for you.

What works

  • Links to recipe websites and blogs. Some sites don't allow reading their recipes; the app then tells you.
  • Links to public Instagram and TikTok posts. ReciPics reads the caption and, if it has no recipe, tries the video for videos up to 2 minutes. Instagram and TikTok change often, so an import can fail or be paused.
  • One link at a time. Sharing a photo or plain text without a link does not start an import; for a photo or screenshot, use screenshot to recipe.

Limits

New installs without a paid plan get 5 free link imports per calendar month. Only imports that work count, and the import screen shows how many are left.

ReciPics reads recipes with AI, and AI can make mistakes. Compare the recipe with the original before you cook, especially amounts and temperatures, and check ingredients yourself for allergies or intolerances.
